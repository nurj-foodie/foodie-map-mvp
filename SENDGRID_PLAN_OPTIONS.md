## 🎯 When You Need Paid Plan

### Domain Authentication (Better Deliverability)

**Why:** Domain authentication (SPF/DKIM) significantly improves inbox placement:
- **Free Tier:** 60-70% inbox placement (with single sender)
- **Domain Auth:** 85-95% inbox placement

**When to upgrade:**
- Moving to production
- Need better deliverability
- Sending >100 emails/day
- Want custom domain emails
