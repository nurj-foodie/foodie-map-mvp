## 💰 TOKEN BONUS: WEEKLY vs MONTHLY

### Option 1: Weekly Token Bonus

**Founder Tier:**
- +10% bonus tokens every week
- Bonus applies to all actions during the week
- Resets every Monday

**Beta Tester:**
- +5% bonus tokens every week
- Bonus applies to all actions during the week
- Resets every Monday

**Pros:**
- ✅ More frequent rewards (feels more valuable)
- ✅ Encourages weekly engagement
- ✅ Users see benefits more often

**Cons:**
- ❌ More complex to implement (weekly tracking)
- ❌ Might feel less special (too frequent)

---

### Option 2: Monthly Token Bonus

**Founder Tier:**
- +10% bonus tokens every month
- Bonus applies to all actions during the month
- Resets on 1st of each month

**Beta Tester:**
- +5% bonus tokens every month
- Bonus applies to all actions during the month
- Resets on 1st of each month

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simpler to implement (monthly tracking)
- ✅ Feels more special (monthly milestone)
- ✅ Easier to communicate to users

**Cons:**
- ❌ Less frequent (might feel less valuable)
- ❌ Users might forget about it

---

### Option 3: One-Time Bonus (Recommended)

**Founder Tier:**
- +10% bonus tokens permanently (always active)
- No reset needed
- Simpler implementation

**Beta Tester:**
- +5% bonus tokens permanently (always active)
- No reset needed
- Simpler implementation

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simplest to implement
- ✅ Always active (no confusion)
- ✅ Feels valuable (permanent perk)

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Less "special" feeling (no weekly/monthly excitement)

---

## 🎯 RECOMMENDATION: **Option 3 (Permanent Bonus)**

**Why:**
1. **Simplest:** No tracking needed, always active
2. **Clear:** Users understand it's permanent
3. **Fair:** Same bonus every day, no confusion
4. **Valuable:** Permanent perk feels meaningful

**Alternative:** If you want more engagement, use **Option 1 (Weekly)** - more frequent rewards encourage weekly check-ins.
