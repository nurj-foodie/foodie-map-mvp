## 💡 PERK CATEGORIES

### 1. Cosmetic Perks (Recognition)

**Purpose:** Visual recognition, no gameplay advantage

**Options:**
- 🎨 **Special Badge Display:** Animated badge, special glow effect
- 🎨 **Profile Badge:** Prominent display on profile
- 🎨 **Profile Frame:** Special border/frame around profile picture
- 🎨 **Username Color:** Special color for username (e.g., gold for Founder, blue for Beta Tester)
- 🎨 **Profile Theme:** Special profile theme/background

**Pros:**
- ✅ Pure recognition (no gameplay impact)
- ✅ Shariah compliant (no gambling, no riba)
- ✅ Fair for all users (no advantage)

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Might not feel valuable enough for some users

---

### 2. Convenience Perks (Time-Saving)

**Purpose:** Save time, not power

**Options:**
- ⚡ **Energy Bonus:** +10 Energy daily (Founder), +5 Energy daily (Beta Tester)
- ⚡ **Energy Regen Boost:** +25% faster regen (Founder), +10% faster regen (Beta Tester)
- 🍽️ **Token Bonus:** +10% bonus tokens from actions (Founder), +5% bonus tokens (Beta Tester)
- 🎯 **Challenge Bonus:** +25% XP/tokens from challenges (Founder), +10% bonus (Beta Tester)
- ⏱️ **Cooldown Reduction:** 10% faster check-in cooldowns (Founder), 5% faster (Beta Tester)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Meaningful value (time-saving)
- ✅ Still fair (F2P can achieve everything)
- ✅ Encourages engagement

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Might feel like pay-to-win if too strong
- ⚠️ Need to balance carefully

---

### 3. Access Perks (Early/Exclusive Features)

**Purpose:** Early access to features, not exclusive access

**Options:**
- 🚀 **Early Feature Access:** Get new features 1-2 weeks before general release
- 🎮 **Beta Features:** Access to beta features (test new gamification systems)
- 📱 **Exclusive UI:** Special UI themes (Founder theme, Beta theme)
- 🎯 **Priority Support:** Faster response to support tickets
- 📊 **Advanced Analytics:** Access to detailed stats/analytics

**Pros:**
- ✅ Feels exclusive and valuable
- ✅ Doesn't affect core gameplay
- ✅ Builds community

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Early access might confuse users
- ⚠️ Need to maintain fairness

---

### 4. Recognition Perks (Social Status)

**Purpose:** Social recognition, community building

**Options:**
- 👑 **Leaderboard Badge:** Special badge on leaderboards
- 🌟 **Profile Highlighting:** Profile appears first in search results
- 🏆 **Special Rank:** "Founder" or "Beta Tester" rank title
- 💬 **Exclusive Chat:** Founder/Beta Tester only chat channel (if social features exist)
- 📢 **Community Badge:** Special mention in community events

**Pros:**
- ✅ Builds community
- ✅ Pure recognition
- ✅ Encourages loyalty

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Social features might not exist yet
- ⚠️ Need to ensure inclusivity
