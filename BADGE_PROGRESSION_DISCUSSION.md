## BADGE PROGRESSION OPTIONS

### Option 1: Faster Early Progress (More Achievable)

**Rationale:** Make early badges easier to achieve, encourage engagement

| Badge | Requirement | Progression |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 🥾 **Explorer I** | Visit 5 eateries | More achievable |
| 🥾 **Explorer II** | Visit 15 eateries | 3x |
| 🥾 **Explorer III** | Visit 30 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer IV** | Visit 60 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer V** | Visit 100 eateries | 1.67x |

**Pros:**
- ✅ Faster early wins (users get first badge quicker)
- ✅ More achievable for casual users
- ✅ Better for short beta period (1-2 months)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Might feel too easy for serious users
- ❌ Less prestigious at higher tiers

---

### Option 2: Slower Early Progress (More Challenging)

**Rationale:** Make badges feel more prestigious and meaningful

| Badge | Requirement | Progression |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 🥾 **Explorer I** | Visit 15 eateries | More challenging |
| 🥾 **Explorer II** | Visit 35 eateries | 2.33x |
| 🥾 **Explorer III** | Visit 75 eateries | 2.14x |
| 🥾 **Explorer IV** | Visit 150 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer V** | Visit 300 eateries | 2x |

**Pros:**
- ✅ More prestigious badges
- ✅ Better for long-term engagement
- ✅ Feels more rewarding

**Cons:**
- ❌ Might feel too hard for casual users
- ❌ Slower early wins (might discourage users)

---

### Option 3: Balanced Progression (Recommended)

**Rationale:** Balance between achievable and challenging

| Badge | Requirement | Progression |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 🥾 **Explorer I** | Visit 10 eateries | Achievable |
| 🥾 **Explorer II** | Visit 25 eateries | 2.5x |
| 🥾 **Explorer III** | Visit 50 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer IV** | Visit 100 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer V** | Visit 200 eateries | 2x |
| 🥾 **Explorer VI** | Visit 500 eateries | 2.5x (optional) |

**Pros:**
- ✅ Good balance (achievable but challenging)
- ✅ Matches current post-beta design
- ✅ Can add tier VI for long-term users

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Might need adjustment based on beta data

---

### Option 4: Exponential Progression (Steeper Curve)

**Rationale:** Each tier feels significantly more challenging

| Badge | Requirement | Progression |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 🥾 **Explorer I** | Visit 10 eateries | Base |
| 🥾 **Explorer II** | Visit 25 eateries | 2.5x |
| 🥾 **Explorer III** | Visit 60 eateries | 2.4x |
| 🥾 **Explorer IV** | Visit 150 eateries | 2.5x |
| 🥾 **Explorer V** | Visit 400 eateries | 2.67x |

**Pros:**
- ✅ More challenging at higher tiers
- ✅ Better for long-term engagement
- ✅ Feels more prestigious

**Cons:**
- ❌ Might be too hard for casual users
- ❌ Slower progression

## CONSIDERING SHORT BETA PERIOD (1-2 MONTHS)

### Beta Context

- **Duration:** 1-2 months (short)
- **Marketing:** Not done yet (limited users)
- **Goal:** Test core mechanics, not full progression

### Recommendation for Beta

**For Beta Testing:**
- Use **Option 1** (Faster Early Progress) OR current progression
- Focus on getting users to Explorer I and II quickly
- Higher tiers (III-V) are less important for beta

**For Post-Beta:**
- Use **Option 3** (Balanced) or adjust based on beta data
- Can refine based on actual user behavior
